# Activity candidates: NSW research (7 Oct 2026)

> **Update, 7 Oct 2026:** the recommended first batch below is written and verified (16 activities; Sculpture by the Sea is prepared as an event instead). Two findings changed: the **MCA is now ticketed** ($25 adult, under-18s free), and the Sydney By Kayak launch is reached from **North Sydney**, not Milsons Point. Evidence per activity is in `docs/activity-verification.md`.

Candidates for tracker **C1.3** (reach 40+ and meet the spec §5 mix) and for the NSW-wide list (D16). Five researchers checked each place against official sources (venue sites, NSW National Parks, councils, Transport for NSW) on 7 Oct 2026. **Nothing here is verified to the C1.1 standard yet:** every candidate still needs the usual check (trip from Central in the Trip Planner, prices, hours, map) before it's written up. "Partly" and "not confirmed" items are listed so they can be shown as *Not yet confirmed* or fixed first.

Weather scores are **S/C/R/H** = sunny / cloudy / rainy / hot 30°+, each 0 skip, 1 OK, 2 great (spec §5 rubric). Travel times are rough unless marked confirmed.

What this means for the product (a decision, not a task): the one-trip-from-Central model (D14) works for Sydney and day trips by train, but breaks down here. Regional entries would need a trip type (day, overnight, multi-day), a main option (train, coach, flight or car) with total time and changes, the local transport at the far end, and an "available months" field, because season (snow, whales, festivals, summer heat) matters more than the weather on the day.

## Existing activities to re-check

- **Leura Village & Gardens** sends people down to Leura Cascades. NSW National Parks lists the Fern Bower tracks at Leura Cascades as closed until 30 Oct 2026; check whether that's the track in the write-up.
- **North Head Lookouts**: 1080 fox baiting at North Head (also Chowder Bay and Middle Head) until 31 Jan 2027. Worth a Heads up line (no dogs).
- **Blue Mountains generally**: Govetts descent, Rodriguez Pass, Blue Pool at Glenbrook (until 27 Nov) and Honeymoon Bridge are closed; a park-wide fire ban runs until 31 Mar 2027.
